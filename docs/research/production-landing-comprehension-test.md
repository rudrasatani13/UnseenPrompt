# Production Landing Comprehension Test

**Status:** Protocol ready; no participant results recorded

**Target:** 12 participants

- 4 software founders
- 4 people who build primarily with coding agents
- 4 experienced software developers

Do not report this test as completed until anonymized responses and dates are recorded below.

## Five-second test

Show the production landing above the fold for five seconds, then hide it. Ask:

1. What do you think this product is being built to do?
2. What information does it keep?
3. What happens after it keeps that information?

Pass threshold: at least 9 of 12 independently communicate the core meaning:

> It keeps project decisions and evidence between coding sessions and prepares the next prompt.

Do not coach participants or count answers that only say “prompt cleaner,” “notes app,” or “AI
coding tool.”

## Objection test

Show the full page and ask:

1. Why would this not simply live in CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md?
2. Which information belongs in the context file?
3. Which information belongs in UnseenPrompt?

Pass threshold: at least 9 of 12 distinguish stable repository instructions from changing project
state.

## CTA test

Ask what will happen after submitting the email form. Pass only when the participant expects:

1. one confirmation email;
2. no weekly newsletter;
3. a later message when a usable build exists.

## Decision rule

- Ship candidate: all three thresholds pass.
- Revise copy: core meaning fails.
- Revise objection section: context-file distinction fails.
- Revise consent copy: CTA expectation fails.
